Converts a document to markdown or plain text: pass a public URL or the file itself as base64, and get back the content with headings, tables and lists preserved, at a fraction of the tokens that rendered pages cost. Use it when a harness has no native reader for the format — .docx, .xlsx, .odt and .numbers rarely have one — when a document is only a URL away, or when a long PDF's text matters…
