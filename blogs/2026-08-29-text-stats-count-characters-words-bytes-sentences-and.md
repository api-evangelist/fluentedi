Measures text the way a form validator, a database column and a model context window each measure it — characters, UTF-8 bytes and estimated tokens are three different numbers, and conflating them is how 'it fits' turns into a truncation bug. Also returns Flesch reading ease and Flesch-Kincaid grade level.
