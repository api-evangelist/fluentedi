Adds years, months, weeks, days, hours, minutes and seconds to an instant. Calendar units (years, months, weeks, days) move wall-clock time and clamp overflow — Jan 31 plus one month is Feb 28 or 29, never Mar 3. Clock units (hours, minutes, seconds) add exact elapsed time and therefore cross DST boundaries.
